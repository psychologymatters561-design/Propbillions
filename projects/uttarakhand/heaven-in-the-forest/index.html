    What we found

    These come from the listing and public sources only. A full Neev Check adds title, approvals and the agreement.

    • Not checked yet
      RERA registration

      No RERA number on the current listing. Ask for it, and check it on the Uttarakhand RERA website, before you pay anything.

    • Ask about this
      Who can buy here

      Uttarakhand's 2025 land law bars outsiders from buying agricultural land in Nainital district and caps residential land at 250 sq m per family. Ask how villa land titles are held.

      Source: Uttarakhand bans outsiders from buying agricultural, horticultural land in 11 districts (ThePrint, 2025) · Uttarakhand Assembly clears bill to restrict outsiders from buying agricultural land (Scroll) (checked 2026-11-05)

    • Clear
      Prices published

      Starting prices are on the listing: ₹45 lakh, ₹85 lakh and ₹1.10 crore.

    • Ask about this
      Carpet vs super area

      The 1 BHK plan prints 570 sq ft but does not say if that is carpet area. Ask for the RERA carpet area.

    • Ask about this
      Payment plan

      40% falls due 45 days after booking. Under Section 13 of the RERA Act a promoter cannot take more than 10% before a registered agreement for sale, so sign and register first.

    • Not checked yet
      Possession date

      No possession date published.

    • Clear
      No promised returns

      The listing mentions holiday-rental potential. We show no rental figures and promise none.

Questions

Is a Neev Check a legal opinion?

No, not unless an advocate signs the legal checks in it. Checks marked "advocate" are done by an advocate; who that is will be named on each report. Until then we say plainly that it is not a legal opinion.

Do you check projects you sell?

Yes, and we show what we find on their pages, including warnings. London Mart, which we list, shows a delivery warning.

How much does it cost?

To confirm with PropBillions. We will state the price here before launch.

How long does it take?

To confirm. Public-record checks take a day or two; title and agreement checks depend on documents from the developer.

What do I need to give you?

The project name, the unit you are looking at, and the cost sheet and draft agreement if you have them.

Can a check say "do not buy"?

Yes. A "Stop" result means do not pay until it is fixed. If it cannot be fixed, we say so.

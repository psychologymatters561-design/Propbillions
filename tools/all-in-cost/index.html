    Rates used, sources and dates

    Checked 2026-11-05. Every rate here is shown in the tools with its source and date. Secondary sources were used where the official notification could not be fetched; each is marked for a check against the official portal before launch.

    • Uttar Pradesh (Noida, Greater Noida): Stamp duty 7%. Women get 1 point off (6%) on homes up to ₹1 crore; joint male and female 6.5%. Registration 1%. UP Govt extends stamp duty concession for women on property worth up to ₹1 crore (Free Press Journal)Stamp Duty and Registration Charges in Uttar Pradesh 2026 (Godrej Capital) Official: igrsup.gov.in
    • Delhi: Stamp duty 6% (men), 4% (women), 5% (joint). Registration 1% plus ₹100 pasting fee. NDMC and Cantonment areas differ. Stamp Duty & Registration Charges in Delhi 2026 (ClearTax)Stamp Duty and Property Registration Charges in Delhi (BankBazaar) Official: revenue.delhi.gov.in
    • Uttarakhand (Kainchi Dham, Nainital district): Stamp duty 5%. Women 3.75% on property up to ₹25 lakh. Registration 2%; sources disagree on the cap (₹25,000 or ₹50,000), so the tool uses the higher one. Stamp duty and registration charges in Uttarakhand (Square Yards)Stamp duty registration charges in Uttarakhand (HomeFirst) Official: registration.uk.gov.in
    • GST: 5% on under-construction homes, 1% for affordable homes, none after a completion certificate. Most sources give 12% with input tax credit for under-construction shops and offices; at least two say 18% after the September 2025 GST changes. Ask the developer for the rate on its tax invoice. GST on Real Estate in 2026 (Rustomjee)GST on under construction property, rates and calculation (HomeFirst)GST for builders, post GST 2.0 (Disytax)
    • Loan to value: RBI caps on loan to value: up to 90% for loans up to ₹30 lakh, 80% up to ₹75 lakh, 75% above that. Home buyers can now get more loan for property up to Rs 30 lakh (Business Standard, 2015)Home loan LTV ratio calculation (HDFC Bank)

10 Things to Consider Before Buying Your First Home

September 09, 2026

Buying your first home is one of the biggest financial decisions you will make. From choosing the right location to understanding your budget, there are several factors that should be considered before making a purchase.

1. Determine Your Budget

Before visiting properties, determine how much you can comfortably afford.

Your budget should include:

  1. Property price
  2. Down payment
  3. Home loan EMI
  4. Registration and stamp duty
  5. Maintenance charges
  6. Property taxes
  7. Interior and renovation costs
  8. Insurance
  9. Utility expenses

A good property is not necessarily the most expensive property. The right property is one that fits comfortably within your long-term financial plan.

2. Choose the Right Location

Location plays a major role in both lifestyle and property appreciation.

When evaluating a location, consider:

  1. Connectivity to major roads
  2. Public transportation
  3. Schools and colleges
  4. Hospitals
  5. Shopping centres
  6. Restaurants and entertainment
  7. Employment hubs
  8. Future infrastructure projects
  9. Security and neighbourhood quality

A property in a well-connected location can provide better convenience and potentially stronger resale value.

3. Research the Developer

If you are purchasing a property from a developer, research the developer's previous projects.

Check:

  1. Previous delivery record
  2. Construction quality
  3. Customer reviews
  4. Legal history
  5. Project approvals
  6. RERA registration
  7. Maintenance standards
  8. Quality of amenities

Never make a property decision based only on brochures or promotional advertisements.

4. Understand the Total Cost

The advertised property price may not represent the final amount you need to pay.

Ask for a complete cost sheet that includes:

CostDescription
Base PricePrice of the property
ParkingParking allocation charges
Club ChargesClubhouse or amenity charges
MaintenanceAdvance maintenance charges
RegistrationGovernment registration charges
Stamp DutyApplicable stamp duty
GSTApplicable tax on eligible properties
Other ChargesAdditional project-specific charges

Understanding the complete cost before booking helps avoid unexpected expenses later.

5. Check the Property Documents

Before purchasing a property, verify the necessary legal documents.

Important documents may include:

  1. Sale deed
  2. Title documents
  3. Approved building plan
  4. Encumbrance certificate
  5. Completion certificate
  6. Occupancy certificate
  7. RERA registration details
  8. Property tax receipts
  9. Development agreement where applicable

For significant purchases, it is advisable to have the documents reviewed by a qualified property lawyer.

6. Compare Home Loan Options

If you are financing your purchase through a home loan, compare different lenders.

Look at:

  1. Interest rate
  2. Processing fee
  3. Loan tenure
  4. Prepayment conditions
  5. EMI amount
  6. Floating vs fixed rates
  7. Other applicable charges

Even a small difference in interest rates can have a significant impact over a long loan tenure.

7. Visit the Property Multiple Times

Never make a final decision after visiting a property only once.

Visit at different times of the day to understand:

  1. Traffic
  2. Noise levels
  3. Natural lighting
  4. Ventilation
  5. Parking availability
  6. Neighbourhood activity
  7. Accessibility

If possible, visit during both weekdays and weekends.

8. Think About Future Appreciation

Real estate should be evaluated from both a lifestyle and investment perspective.

Factors that may influence future property demand include:

  1. New highways
  2. Metro connectivity
  3. Business districts
  4. Commercial developments
  5. Schools and universities
  6. Healthcare facilities
  7. Shopping and entertainment
  8. Government infrastructure projects

However, future appreciation is never guaranteed, so investment decisions should not rely solely on projected returns.



9. Evaluate Amenities

Modern residential projects often provide a wide range of amenities.

Popular amenities include:

  1. Swimming pool
  2. Gymnasium
  3. Clubhouse
  4. Children's play area
  5. Landscaped gardens
  6. Walking tracks
  7. Sports facilities
  8. Security systems
  9. Multipurpose halls
  10. Co-working spaces

Consider whether you will actually use the amenities rather than paying a premium simply because they are advertised.



10. Think Long Term

A home should ideally meet your needs for several years.

Consider questions such as:

Will the home be large enough if my family grows?

Is the location convenient for work and education?

Will the property be easy to sell or rent in the future?

Are the maintenance costs affordable?

Does the neighbourhood have good long-term potential?



Final Thoughts

Buying a home requires careful research, financial planning and legal due diligence.

Do not rush because of sales pressure or limited-time offers. Compare properties, understand the complete cost, verify documentation and choose a property that fits your financial goals and lifestyle.

The right real estate decision is not simply about buying a beautiful property—it is about making a well-informed long-term decision.



Frequently Asked Questions


Q. What is the most important factor when buying property?

Location, legal documentation, affordability and the quality of the property should all be considered together.

Q. Should I buy a ready-to-move property or an under-construction property?

Both have advantages and disadvantages. A ready property allows you to inspect the finished product, while an under-construction property may provide more options and potentially different pricing.

Q. Should I hire a property lawyer?

For a significant property purchase, professional legal review can help identify title, approval or documentation issues before you commit.

Q. Is real estate always a good investment?

No investment is guaranteed. Property performance depends on location, demand, pricing, financing costs, infrastructure and broader market conditions.

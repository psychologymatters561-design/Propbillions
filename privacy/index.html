Privacy notice

Draft for PropBillions' review. Last updated 5 Nov 2026. To be checked by a lawyer before launch.

This notice explains what personal data PropBillions (PropBillions Pvt Ltd) collects through this website, why, and your rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

What we collect

  • What you type in our forms: name, mobile number, email (optional), budget, preferred visit slot and any note.
  • The page you sent it from and campaign tags in the link (for example utm_source).
  • No data is collected by the calculators. They run in your browser.

Why we use it

  • To answer the request you made: a call back, a shortlist, a site visit, a brochure or a Neev Check.
  • Only if you tick the second box: to send you new project updates.
  • Only with that same marketing consent: to tell Meta and Google, in hashed form, that an enquiry happened, so we can measure our ads.

Who sees it

The PropBillions team. We do not share your number with a developer without asking you first. Service providers that carry the message (for example our email or messaging provider) process it on our behalf: to confirm.

How long we keep it

to confirm. You can ask us to delete it sooner.

Your rights

  • Withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it.
  • Ask what we hold, correct it, or have it erased.
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights for you.
  • Complain to our grievance officer, and then to the Data Protection Board of India.

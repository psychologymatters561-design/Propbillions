    NRI guide

    Buying from abroad, without flying in for every step.

    A plain summary to plan with. It is not legal or tax advice: confirm with your bank and a chartered accountant. Which NRI markets PropBillions serves is to confirm.

    1. 01

      Who can buy

      NRIs and OCI cardholders can buy residential and commercial property in India. Agricultural land, plantation property and farmhouses need special permission. Confirm your case with your bank.

    2. 02

      How to pay

      Through normal banking channels: an NRE, NRO or FCNR account in India, or an inward remittance. No cash, no traveller’s cheques.

    3. 03

      Home loan

      Indian banks lend to NRIs. Expect them to ask for overseas income proof and a co-applicant or local power of attorney.

    4. 04

      Power of attorney

      If you cannot be here for registration, a registered power of attorney can sign for you. Keep it specific to this property.

    5. 05

      Tax when you sell

      The buyer deducts tax at source when an NRI sells. Plan for it before you buy, not after.

    6. 06

      Uttarakhand

      The 2025 land law limits what people from outside the state can buy there. Read our note before a hill purchase.
